THE GOLDEN BLOOM HONEY GUIDE

Good to Know.
Lovely to Enjoy.

A few simple habits to make the most of every jar.

01 / KEEP IT WELL

Close the lid. Keep it dry.

Store honey in a tightly closed jar, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Use a clean, dry spoon each time.

02 / FIND YOUR PAIRING

Start with a little drizzle.

Try honey with warm toast, yoghurt, fruit or a homemade dressing. Add a little, taste, and adjust to your preference.

03 / NOTICE THE TEXTURE

Every jar has its moments.

Honey can develop crystals over time. Texture alone does not establish its purity; refer to the product label for care and quality information.
